f(n) = sqrt(n)
g(n) = log(n)
h(n) = f(n) + g(n) = n^(1/2) + log(n)
-------
log(n^(1/2)) = 1/2 * log(n)
log(n) = 2 log(n^(1/2))

for each n > 100:
  n^(1/2) > 10
then:
  for each n > 100:
    log(n^(1/2)) < n^(1/2)
then:
  for each n > 100:
    h(n) < 3 * n^(1/2) 
---------------
then:
  h(n) = O(n^(1/2)) = O(sqrt(n))
